# Common mistakes when coding against the Sage Intacct XML API

Use as a review checklist. Grep a request or codebase for the pattern in "Looks like", then apply the rule. The
last column names the file and section that backs the rule.

| # | Looks like | Why it is wrong | Rule (reference) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `GET https://api.intacct.com/ia/xml/xmlgw.phtml?...`, or `Content-Type: application/json` with a JSON body | The gateway takes HTTP POST of an XML document only; GET has been blocked since 15 January 2023 and JSON is not accepted. | `api-basics.md` § 2 |
| 2 | `<dtdversion>2.1</dtdversion>` on new work, or no `dtdversion` | 3.0 is the current version and the only one that offers the generic functions; 2.1 is not enhanced. | `api-basics.md` § 1, § 3.1 |
| 3 | A `login` block (user ID, company ID, password) in every request | Not recommended: call `getAPISession` once, then send `sessionid` and use the **endpoint the session returned**. | `api-basics.md` § 3.3, § 4 |
| 4 | `getAPISession` in the same request as other functions | The portal says to isolate it in its own request. | `api-basics.md` § 4 |
| 5 | Posting every request to `api.intacct.com` after obtaining a session | A session is tied to a unique endpoint; use the `endpoint` from the `getAPISession` response. | `api-basics.md` § 4 |
| 6 | Trusting a session ID received from a Smart Event, trigger or browser without checking | Validate it with `getAPISession` and check the endpoint host is `*.intacct.com`. | `api-basics.md` § 4 |
| 7 | Sender ID, sender password, user password or session IDs in source, logs or examples | Credentials; the sender ID is also authorised per company and audited. Use placeholders and secret storage. | `api-basics.md` § 2 |
| 8 | Same `controlid` attribute on every `function`, or none | The function control ID is what the gateway uses to recover from mid-process failures and to enforce idempotence; use a GUID or sequence per function. | `api-basics.md` § 3.4, § 5.3 |
| 9 | Retrying a request that got no response with `uniqueid` `false` (or the default left off a write) | Some functions may already have run. Resubmit the identical request with `<uniqueid>true</uniqueid>` so already-processed functions are skipped. | `api-basics.md` § 5.3 |
| 10 | Checking only the HTTP status, or only `control/status` | There are three status levels (control, authentication, each function's result); a 200 with `<result><status>failure</status>` is an error. Read `errormessage/error/errorno` and `description2`. | `api-basics.md` § 5.1, § 5.2 |
| 11 | Treating `GW-0010` (HTTP 429) as fatal, or hammering the gateway in a polling loop | 429 is the client's rate limit; back off and retry until success; polling and concurrent loops are the named causes. | `api-basics.md` § 5.2, § 10 |
| 12 | 1,000 creates in one request, or one request per record in parallel | Keep writes under about 100 affected records and queries under 1,000 records per request; the sender times out after 15 minutes; concurrency is limited per tenant and excess requests are queued. | `api-basics.md` § 10 |
| 13 | `transaction="true"` around a large batch | Rollback of a large multi-function operation is expensive; keep transactional operations small. | `api-basics.md` § 3.2, § 10 |
| 14 | `<query>` with `<select><field>*</field></select>`, or no `select` | `select` is required and "Returning all fields is not supported" in `query`. | `api-basics.md` § 7.1; `functions.md` `query` |
| 15 | `<pagesize>5000</pagesize>` | `query` allows 1 to 2000 (default 100); `readByQuery` 1 to 1000 (default 100). | `api-basics.md` § 7.1, § 7.2; `functions.md` |
| 16 | Paging `query` with `offset` and expecting a stable snapshot | Each offset call is a fresh query; inserts between calls can shift rows and skip records. Order by a stable field and reconcile, or use `readByQuery` + `readMore` with `resultId` for a fixed result set. | `api-basics.md` § 7.1, § 7.2 |
| 17 | `readMore` with `<object>` while several `readByQuery` calls on that object are in flight | Use the `resultId` from the `readByQuery` response so the pages come from the same result set. | `api-basics.md` § 7.2 |
| 18 | `readByQuery` filtering on `PERSONALINFO.LASTNAME` or another dotted field | Joined fields cannot be filtered with `readByQuery`; use `query` with the `OBJECTPATH.FIELD` notation. | `api-basics.md` § 7.2; FAQ |
| 19 | `query` `select` of `CUSTOMER.PARENT.PARENT.NAME`, or across a custom relationship between two standard objects | One hierarchical level only; custom relationships only when at least one side is a custom object. | `api-basics.md` § 7.1 |
| 20 | `readByQuery` expected to return a transaction's lines, or a one-to-many relationship | It cannot; get the keys, then `read` the header (owned objects come nested). | `api-basics.md` § 7.2, § 5.4 |
| 21 | `WHENCREATED = '2026-10-13'`, `2026-10-13T00:00:00Z`, or a local-time conversion | Dates are `mm/dd/yyyy` strings (generic) or year/month/day objects (legacy); ISO 8601 is not supported and dates carry no time zone. | `api-basics.md` § 8 |
| 22 | `<TRX_AMOUNT>1,234.567</TRX_AMOUNT>` | No thousands separators; amounts beyond two decimals are rounded, round client-side. | `api-basics.md` § 8 |
| 23 | `query` filter `STATE = 'Posted'` on a bill, copied from a `read` | Validated lists store single characters (`STATE='A'` for a posted bill in `readByQuery`); `lookup` lists the valid values and the object page's `query` fields table lists the database characters. | `api-basics.md` § 7.2; `objects/accounts-payable.md` (Bills, readByQuery query fields) |
| 24 | Updating one line of a sales order by sending only that line in a generic `update` of the header, or `update` on `SODOCUMENTENTRY` | Lines are owned objects: write them through the header. For OE/PO/IC transactions the documented update is the legacy `update_sotransaction` / `update_potransaction` / `update_ictransaction` with `updatesotransitem line_num`. | `api-basics.md` § 6; `oe-po-inventory.md` § 3 |
| 25 | `line_num` taken straight from the `LINE_NO` of a `read` on `SODOCUMENT`, `PODOCUMENT` or `INVDOCUMENT` | `read` numbers those lines from 0, the legacy update functions from 1: add one. The portal says to test before any large update. | `api-basics.md` § 6 |
| 26 | `<update><GLBATCH>` with only the changed entry | A statistical journal update replaces all entries; send every entry to keep. | `api-basics.md` § 6 (statistical journals); `objects/general-ledger.md` |
| 27 | Hard-coding `transactiontype` `Sales Order` / `Purchase Order` for every client, or inventing one | The transaction definition name is company configuration; read `SODOCUMENTPARAMS` / `PODOCUMENTPARAMS` / `INVDOCUMENTPARAMS` or ask. | `oe-po-inventory.md` § 1 |
| 28 | Reading `SODOCUMENT` / `PODOCUMENT` / `INVDOCUMENT` without `docparid` and wondering where the custom fields went | `docparid` (the transaction definition) is required to see a definition's custom fields in `query`, `read`, `readByQuery` and `lookup`. | `api-basics.md` § 7.1; `oe-po-inventory.md` § 2 |
| 29 | Storing the `RECORDNO` of the AR invoice or AP bill that an OE/PO transaction generated | Updating the OE/PO transaction deletes and recreates the AR/AP document with a **new** `RECORDNO`; link through `PRRECORDKEY` (OE/PO side) or filter AP bills by `MODULEKEY` `9.PO`. | `oe-po-inventory.md` § 4; `objects/accounts-payable.md` (Bills intro) |
| 30 | Transaction lines without `locationid` on a multi-entity company | "Required if company is multi-entity enabled" on OE and Purchasing lines; AP bills also need `LOCATIONID` per line at the top level. | `api-basics.md` § 9; `objects/order-entry.md`, `objects/purchasing.md` |
| 31 | Querying from a top-level session and missing private-entity records | Set `options/showprivate` to `true`, or sign in to the entity. | `api-basics.md` § 7.1, § 9 |
| 32 | Adding to a customer's contact list through generic `create`/`update` of `CUSTOMER` | Not supported there; the legacy `create_customer` / `update_customer` do it. | FAQ; `objects/accounts-receivable.md` (Customers) |
| 33 | `<ATTACHMENTS>` or `<SUPDOC>` on a transaction | The field is `SUPDOCID`; attachments and folders are managed with the legacy `create_supdoc` family. | `api-basics.md` § 8; `objects/company-console.md` (Attachments) |
| 34 | `TAXENTRIES` / `INCLUSIVETAX` on a company outside AU, GB or ZA | The portal scopes inclusive tax and tax entries to AU, GB and ZA companies. | `objects/accounts-payable.md` (Bills, create notes) |
| 35 | Pinning Sage's TLS certificate or CA file in the client | Breaks at CloudFlare certificate rotations; use the platform's certificate store. | `api-basics.md` § 2 |
| 36 | Inventing an object, field, function or enum value | Every object and operation comes from `objects.md`; every field name, data type, valid value and relationship from the 2026 Release 3 snapshot (`snapshot/`, as the API reports them) or, for what a create or update accepts, from the object page's parameter tables; every generic function and its grammar from `functions.md`, every legacy function name from its list. A field in the snapshot but absent from the portal tables is real (newer than the page), not invented; anything in neither source must be confirmed with `lookup` / `inspect` on the client's company. | `INDEX.md`; `SKILL.md` § 2 |
